Privacy Policy

Pentool is a local tool: traffic, projects and findings stay on your machine. This page lists everything that leaves it, why, and how to turn it off.

Last updated 24 November 2026

Short version. We never receive your traffic, requests, responses, findings or project databases. Buying a licence gives us your e-mail and key data. Crash reports can be switched off.

1. Who is responsible

Data controller: Anatoly Kashtanov (DoctorX), author of Pentool. Any privacy request: support@pentool.pro.

2. What the app sends, and when

WhatWhenExactly whatCan it be off?
Licence validationAt startup with a PRO key, then cachedKey string, hashed machine id, app versionNot while using PRO
Install pingOnce per installationRandom install id, OS and version — no traffic, no personal dataYes, in the settings
Crash reportOnly on a fatal crashTraceback, error type, version, OS and architecture, source (tui or cli)Yes — send_crash_reports: false

Token-like strings are stripped from tracebacks before sending. Reports are stored on the licence service, which runs on Cloudflare Workers.

3. The AI runs locally

Pentool's AI features are served by a local model that runs as a separate process on your machine. Requests, responses and findings are not sent to us or to any cloud service. The only network access is the one-time download of the model file, if you enable it.

4. What we do not collect

  • proxied requests and responses, cookies, credentials, session tokens;
  • findings, site maps, project databases or files from your disk;
  • keystrokes, clipboard, screen contents or terminal history;
  • analytics or advertising identifiers on this site.

5. Buying a licence

When you buy PRO we process:

  • e-mail — the key goes there, and it names the licence for support and renewals;
  • number of slots, amount and payment date — for accounting and renewals;
  • payment data — handled by the provider Coinso and the payment networks; we never see wallet private keys or card numbers.

The legal basis is performance of the contract (delivering the licence) and accounting obligations. Purchase records are kept as long as tax law requires.

6. Activation data

Each activation stores the key and a hashed machine identifier on the licence service, so that used slots can be counted and the licence moved between machines. The identifier is a hash of the system machine id; it is not used to identify a person.

7. Data on your machine

Pentool writes to ~/.config/pentool/: settings, licence cache, logs, certificates for HTTPS interception, the project database and plugins. The AI model lives in ~/.pentool/ai/models. This data does not leave the machine unless you send it. Deleting the directory deletes all of it.

8. Cookies and this site

This site is static: it sets no cookies and loads no analytics or third-party scripts. Payment pages belong to the provider and follow its own rules.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, 152-FZ and others) you may request access to your data, its correction, deletion or export, object to processing and complain to a supervisory authority. Write to support@pentool.pro — we answer within 30 days.

10. Retention

  • crash reports — up to 12 months;
  • purchase and licence records — for the licence term plus the period required by tax law;
  • install counter — aggregate only, no per-user records.

11. Children

Pentool is a professional security tool and is not intended for people under 18.

12. Changes to this policy

A new version is published here with a new date and applies from publication.

13. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: support@pentool.pro.
