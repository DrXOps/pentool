Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Pentool is a local terminal tool: your traffic, projects and scan results stay on your machine. This policy explains the few things that leave it, why, and how to turn them off.

Short version. We never receive your proxied traffic, requests, responses, scan results or project databases. If you buy a licence we receive your e-mail and the key data. If the tool crashes it may send an anonymous crash report — you can disable that.

1. Who controls your data

Data controller: Anatoly Kashtanov (DoctorX), publisher of Pentool. Contact for any privacy request: support@pentool.pro.

2. What the tool sends, and when

WhatWhenWhat exactlyCan it be off?
Licence validation At startup with a PRO key, then cached Key string, hashed machine id, app version Not while using PRO features
First-run ping Once per installation (install counter) Random install id, OS and version — no traffic, no user data Yes, via config
Crash report Only when the app crashes fatally Traceback, exception type, app version, OS/arch, source (tui/cli) Yes — send_crash_reports: false
AI features (optional) Only when you use AI analysis / AI recon The prompt you trigger: request fragments or scan context you chose to send to the configured AI provider Yes — AI is off by default, provider is yours

Tracebacks are scrubbed of token-like strings (long hex sequences) before sending. Reports are stored on our licence service, which is hosted on Cloudflare Workers.

3. What we do not collect

  • proxied HTTP requests and responses, cookies, credentials, session tokens;
  • scan findings, site maps, project databases, files from your disk;
  • keystrokes, clipboard, screen contents, terminal history;
  • analytics, advertising identifiers, third-party trackers on this site.

4. Purchases

When you buy a PRO licence we (through the payment provider CryptoProc) process:

  • e-mail address — used to deliver the key and as the key's name for support and renewals;
  • number of slots and the plan/amount;
  • payment data — handled by CryptoProc and the payment networks; we do not see card numbers or wallet private keys.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (delivering the licence) and legal obligations (accounting). We keep purchase records as long as required by tax law.

5. Activation data

Each activation stores the key and the hashed machine identifier on the licence service so that the number of used slots can be counted and the licence can be moved between machines. The machine identifier is a hash derived from your system's machine id — it is not used to identify you personally.

6. Local data on your machine

Pentool writes to ~/.config/pentool/: configuration, licence cache, logs, crash fallback files, certificates for HTTPS interception and your project databases. This data never leaves your machine unless you send it. Delete the directory to remove all of it.

7. Cookies and this website

This website is static: it sets no cookies, loads no analytics and no third-party scripts. The payment provider's checkout pages are separate and are governed by their own privacy policy.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR, Russian 152-ФЗ) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Write to support@pentool.pro; we answer within 30 days.

9. Retention

  • crash reports: up to 12 months;
  • purchase and licence records: for the licence term plus the period required by tax law;
  • install counter: aggregate only, no per-user record.

10. Children

Pentool is a professional security tool and is not directed at persons under 18.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the new version is published here with a new date. The change applies from publication.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and requests: support@pentool.pro.
