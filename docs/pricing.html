Payment

How buying works

You choose the number of machines and leave an e-mail. Then the Coinso payment page opens — cryptocurrency only, no cards or bank transfers.

01

Pick the slots

One slot is one machine. The price updates right away: $29 a year for the first slot, +$19 for each extra one.

02

Leave an e-mail

The key goes to that address. It also names the licence for support, which makes orders easy to find.

03

Pay in cryptocurrency

Coinso issues an invoice with the exact amount and network. The key is generated automatically after payment.

04

Activate the key

Settings → License → Activate. The first activation takes a slot and binds it to that machine.

No key within half an hour? Write to support@pentool.pro with the order number and e-mail — we will issue it by hand.

Licence

Slots, moving machines and offline work

  • Slots. The number of machines is fixed at purchase. Activating on a new machine takes a free slot; deactivating frees it.
  • Moving. Deactivate the key on the old machine, or ask support to move the binding.
  • Offline. The key is validated at startup and cached. Without a network, PRO keeps working for up to 7 days.
  • Expiry. PRO modules stop opening, the free part keeps working. The key and its slots stay for renewal.

Questions

The short answers

Do I need an account for the free version?
No. uv tool install pentool and you are working. No e-mail, no account, no expiry.
What does a PRO key unlock?
The vulnerability scanner, reconnaissance, the local AI, reports with exports and Intruder Turbo. The table above is the complete list.
Can I try PRO before buying?
Write to support — we will set up a 14-day trial by hand.
How many machines can I activate?
As many as you bought slots. One slot is one machine; when you move, the slot is released on the old one.
What happens after a year?
PRO modules close, everything else keeps working. The key and slots stay — renewal brings the access back.
Does Pentool send data to a server?
Only service data: the licence check at startup (the key and a hashed machine id), a one-time install ping and crash reports. Traffic, findings and projects stay on your machine; the AI runs locally. Crash reports can be switched off in the settings.
What is the refund policy?
Full refund within 14 days of purchase — write to support@pentool.pro from the purchase e-mail with the key. The key is disabled and its slots released.
Is there a team plan?
There is no separate plan. If you need several machines, buy a key with the number of slots you need.
